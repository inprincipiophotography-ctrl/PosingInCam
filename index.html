FAQ

      
Good to know.

      

        
Will it work on my camera?
Tested on Sony A7 III/IV/V, Canon R6/R6 II/R6 III and Nikon Z6/Z8/Z9, and it works on most recent Sony Alpha, Canon EOS R and Nikon Z bodies. Pick your brand in the tool above. Fujifilm and Panasonic support is coming soon. If something isn't working on your camera, let us know and we'll help.

        
Is it safe for my camera and SD card?
Yes. You only copy ordinary JPEG files to the card, the same kind your camera already makes. Nothing is installed and your existing photos are untouched.

        
Why won't a normal image show on my camera?
Cameras only display files saved in their own format: the right size, the right metadata and the right folder. We convert any image to match, so it appears in playback like a photo you shot.

        
Do I need an account?
Yes, you sign in with a one-time email link, no password. The free plan includes 3 images with a small watermark; paid plans remove it.

        
What's the difference between the tool and the pose packs?
The tool converts your own images (designs, shot lists, anything). The pose packs are 50 ready-made wedding poses we shot and designed, sold separately in our store.

        
Anything special per camera?
Sony needs one extra step (Recover Image Database), which is in the included instructions. Canon and Nikon are essentially plug and play.

        
Who made this?
We're Kim & Ivan, the wedding photographers behind In Principio. We built this for our own shoots and opened it up to other photographers.

Which camera is in your hand?

Cameras only show files in their own format. We save your card exactly the way your camera saves a photo.

Tested on Sony A7 III/IV/V, Canon R6 II/R6 III and Nikon Z8/Z9.

SD card

Copy it to your SD card.

You formatted the card in the camera and took one test photo, so the camera made its own folder. Now drop your card in.

  1. Drag DSC00001.JPG into 100MSDCF on the SD card.
  2. Eject the card before you pull it out.

Use a card reader, not a USB cable to the camera.

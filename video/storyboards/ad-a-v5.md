# Ad A v5 "Dark glass": storyboard

18.5 s · 1080 × 1920 · 30 fps · 555 frames · 120 BPM
Code: `src/v5/AdA5.tsx`. Clock: `src/v5/timeline.json`; movement timings: `src/v5/moves.ts`. Sound: `ad-a-v5-sound.md`.
Ad A v4 is kept unchanged (`AdA4-9x16`, render in `renders/AdA4-9x16.mp4`).

The look comes from `inspo/`, in NotaLucis red:
- **Glass.** Frosted panels (`src/v5/Glass.tsx`) with a blurred backdrop, a hairline edge that is light at the top left, and a red glow from below.
- **Stage.** A near-black stage (`#07060a`) with a slow bordo glow, and the footage graded cooler and darker towards the edges.
- **Type.** Medium white Inter with one word in big Instrument Serif italic, light red (`#ff6a5c`), softly glowing (`src/v5/Type.tsx`, write it as `*word*`). Lines rise out of a mask 4 frames apart.
- **Safe zone.** Everything sits inside Instagram's safe area: below the top 14 %, above the bottom 35 %.

**1 · Hook (POV)** · 0:00–3.0
- The wedding take over her shoulder, the card on the camera in her hands.
- The caption "POV: your camera tells you / the *next pose.*" is fully set on the first frame.
- At 0.3 a glass notification drops in at the top: a thumbnail of the card, "NEXT POSE · Editorial 1/1", and "“Stand in front of him, pop the leg…”".

**2 · The couple** · 3.0–5.0
- A hard cut with a punch-in to the couple, wide as in v4. The photographer's shoulder falls into the dark bottom.
- Caption: "Glance. Pose. *Shoot.*"

**3 · The card** · 5.0–11.0
- 5.0–5.8: the Editorial 1/1 card (vector, sharp) rises out of the dark, its tilt settling, with a red rim light under it. Title: "Everything you need, / on *one card.*"
- The three glass callouts slide in from the right, 0.8 s apart. Each draws a glowing red line to its part of the card:
  - 6.1: **THE POSE**, "Reference photo to copy", points to the photo.
  - 6.9: **THE HACK**, "His hand must be visible holding her.", points to the hack.
  - 7.7: **SAY TO THE COUPLE**, the quote, points to the box.
  - They are ordered top to bottom by what they point at, so the lines never cross.
- 8.95: the callouts leave.
- 9.2–10.0: the card moves to the centre and shrinks. Four pack cards fan out behind it, 3 frames apart. Caption: "Every pose for / *the whole day.*"

**4 · Playback** · 11.0–14.0
- The 4K clip of a thumb scrolling playback, the camera centred in the safe area.
- Caption: "Right in your camera's / *playback.*"
- Glass chips "Sony · Canon · Nikon" pop in under the caption at 12.3, clear of the screen.

**5 · End** · 14.0–18.5
- A red orb glows up on the dark stage.
- 14.1: the mark as a glowing tile, then "Shoot Cards by NotaLucis".
- 14.45: "Never go *blank* / on a shoot again."

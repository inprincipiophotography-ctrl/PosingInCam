# Shoot Cards Meta ads — storyboards

Three 13-second ads, each in 9:16 (Reels/Stories, 1080 × 1920) and 4:5 (Feed, 1080 × 1350), 30 fps, 390 frames.
Code: `src/Ads.tsx`. Sound-off first: every line is on screen. All three end on the same 2.7 s end card (10.3–13.0 s, frames 309–390): logo, "Never go *blank* on a shoot again.", "Make my free card →", cards.notalucis.com.
Test them against each other; the one that wins on cost per landing-page view gets the budget.

## Ad A · "Blank"

**1 · Hook** — 0:00–3.6, frames 0–108
- "The couple’s waiting." / "Your mind goes *blank*." Word by word, slow push-in.

**2 · Playback** — 3.6–8.1, frames 108–243
- The real camera, thumb scrolling cards, full-bleed. Caption: "Press ▶. Your poses are *right there.*"

**3 · Words to say** — 8.1–10.3, frames 243–309
- Push in on The First Look card; ring on the "Say to the couple" box. Caption: "With the exact words to say".

**4 · End card** — 10.3–13.0

## Ad B · "No phone"

**1 · Phone** — 0:00–3.6, frames 0–108
- Phone scrolling a feed of poses. "Still hunting for poses / in front of the *couple?*"

**2 · Turn** — 3.6–5.0, frames 108–150
- "No phone." / "No *Pinterest.*"

**3 · Playback** — 5.0–8.6, frames 150–258
- Stop motion: First Look → Editorial → Vogue → Fit Check, 0.9 s each, every card lined up on the same spot so only the card changes. Caption: "Just your camera’s *playback.*"

**4 · More cards** — 8.6–10.3, frames 258–309
- Camera back: Timeline → Family groups → Shot list. Caption: "Timeline. Family groups. *Shot list.*"

**5 · End card** — 10.3–13.0

## Ad C · "Who’s next?"

**1 · Hook** — 0:00–3.2, frames 0–96
- "Who’s *next?*" with questions popping in around it: Grandma Rose? · His parents? · Her siblings? · Uncle Tom? · Both families?

**2 · The list in playback** — 3.2–8.4, frames 96–252
- Camera back, the Family groups card; ▶ to Timeline (5.6 s), ▶ to Shot list (7.1 s). Caption: "Your family list, *in your camera.*"

**3 · Turn** — 8.4–10.3, frames 252–309
- "No phone." / "No *guessing.*"

**4 · End card** — 10.3–13.0

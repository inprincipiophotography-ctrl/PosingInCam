# Ad A v2 "Blank" — storyboard

16.0 s · 1080 × 1920 · 30 fps · 480 frames · 120 BPM (1 beat = 0.5 s = 15 frames)
Code: `src/v2/AdA2.tsx`. Clock shared with the music: `src/v2/timeline.json` → `scripts/music.py`.
Rules: `launch-motion` skill + `references/relay-motion-skill.md`. Text stays out of the top 14 % and bottom 35 % (Instagram UI).

Look: real photos and footage full-bleed in every shot; big Instrument Serif in white with a red italic accent; small Inter caps labels like the cards' own ("SAY TO THE COUPLE"); bordo panel wipes (the strip on every pose card); film grain and vignette. No empty backgrounds, no vector props.

**1 · Hook** — 0:00–4.0, frames 0–120 (bars 1–2, intro)
- On screen: an out-of-focus, darkened close-up of a camera screen. "The couple’s / waiting." (0.15 s), then "Your mind / goes" (2.0 s).
- Motion: lines rise out of masks, 4 frames apart; the first sentence exits upward at 1.8 s.
- Camera: slow push-in.
- 3.5 s: the frame flashes white; "*blank.*" in huge red italic. The music cuts to a faint ring.

**2 · Drop / playback** — 4.0–6.5, frames 120–195 (bar 3)
- On screen: the real camera, a thumb scrolling playback (clip 0–2.3 s). Label SHOOT CARDS; "Your poses. / *In playback.*"
- Motion: the white flash punches through into the clip on the drop; punch-in zooms on the bar lines (4.0 s, 6.0 s).
- 6.25 s: bordo wipe up; covered on the beat at 6.5 s.

**3 · Stop motion** — 6.5–8.0, frames 195–240
- On screen: Editorial → Vogue → Fit Check, a cut on every beat. Each card is lined up on the same spot, so only the card changes; a shutter click on each cut.
- Text: "Right in your / *camera.*" with one label per cut: THE POSE · CAMERA SETTINGS · A POSING HACK.
- Camera: one slow push across all the cuts.

**4 · Payoff** — 8.0–11.6, frames 240–348 (bars 5–6)
- On screen: The First Look (full-size photo), framed exactly like the stop motion. At 8.5 s an eased push into its "Say to the couple" box, which stays readable on the camera's own screen.
- Text: label ON EVERY CARD; "The exact words / to say to *them.*" (9.25 s), held about 2 s.

**5 · No phone** — 11.35–13.0, frames 340–390
- On screen: cross-zoom into the clip (2.9 s onwards), darkened. "No phone. / No *Pinterest.*" (11.5 s).
- 12.75 s: bordo wipe; covered at 13.0 s.

**6 · Logo + CTA** — 13.0–16.0, frames 390–480 (bar 8)
- On screen: bordo. White mark builds; "Shoot Cards" / "by NotaLucis" follow 4 frames apart. "Never go *blank* / on a shoot again." White button "Make my free card →" (13.45 s) with one press on the beat at 14.5 s; cards.notalucis.com · First card free · no account.
- Readable for 2.5 s or more; the whole lock-up drifts to the last frame.

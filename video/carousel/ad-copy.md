# Carousel C: Meta ad (Instagram + Facebook)

Run it from the In Principio Weddings page **as an ad only**, not as a post on the profile. In Ads Manager, create the ad there (not with "Boost post"), so it never appears in the grid and your couples don't see it. Target wedding photographers.

- **Cards:** `renders/carousel/Cad-4x5/01.jpg … 08.jpg` (1080 × 1350, in this order).
- **Button:** Learn more.
- **Website URL (every card):** https://cards.notalucis.com/?utm_source=meta&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign=carousel_c
- **Display link:** cards.notalucis.com

## Settings that help

- **Objective:** Traffic (landing page views) to start. Switch to Sales/Leads once the site tracks sign-ups with the Meta pixel.
- **Placements:** Instagram Feed, Instagram Explore, Facebook Feed. Run Reels and Stories with the video (`renders/AdA8-9x16.mp4`).
- **Card order:** turn **off** "Automatically show the best-performing cards first". The cards tell a story in order.
- **Audience:** photographers, ideally in the countries you sell to, with interests such as Wedding photography, Photographer, Sony Alpha, Canon, Nikon, Adobe Lightroom. Exclude people who already follow In Principio Weddings, so couples don't see it.
- **Budget:** a small test (e.g. €10–15 a day for 5–7 days), then compare cost per landing page view against the video ad and keep the cheaper one.

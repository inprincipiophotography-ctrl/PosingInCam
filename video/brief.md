# Shoot Cards — 30-second launch video + Meta ads

**Company:** Shoot Cards (cards.notalucis.com), a NotaLucis tool, puts pose cards, timelines, family-group lists and shot lists in the camera's own playback. Each card has the reference photo, the words to say to the couple, camera settings and a posing hack. Made by Kim & Ivan, wedding photographers at In Principio.

**Audience:** wedding and portrait photographers shooting Sony, Canon or Nikon, solo or small studios, international (English).

**Goal:** launch video for the website, YouTube and socials; three short paid ads for Instagram and Facebook (Reels, Stories, Feed).

**One message:** your poses live in the camera now, not on your phone.

**Must show:**
1. The hook: the couple waits, your mind goes blank.
2. The problem: reaching for the phone and scrolling for poses in front of the couple.
3. The product: the real camera, a thumb scrolling playback from card to card.
4. The payoff: one card up close, with the pose, the words to say, settings and a hack.
5. Logo + CTA: "Make my free card →", cards.notalucis.com, first card free, no account.

**Tone:** a working photographer talking to another one. Calm, premium, the NotaLucis look (light, dot grid, one red accent, Inter + Instrument Serif). No voiceover; music + SFX only, every line on screen for sound-off viewing.

**Rules (PRODUCT.md):** show the real thing. No stock imagery, no AI footage, no invented testimonials, user counts or ratings. Only cards whose photos are In Principio's own (or cleared in writing).

**Deliverables:**
- Launch: 30 s, 16:9 1920×1080 60 fps master, plus 9:16 1080×1920 and 4:5 1080×1350.
- Ads A, B, C: 13 s each, 9:16 and 4:5, 30 fps.

**Assets:** `video/source/` (8 camera photos, 27 MP; the playback-scroll clip, 4K), `assets/showcase/pack-*.jpg` (pose-pack cards), text cards drawn by `assets/builder.js`, logo `favicon.svg`, fonts in `video/public/fonts/`.

**Still to come:** a licensed music track cleared for paid ads; footage of the phone moment, a real shoot and the SD card (the problem scene is motion graphics until then).

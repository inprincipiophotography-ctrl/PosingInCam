THESIS: The page is one frosted pane on marshmallow, lit from its lower left by a Bubblegum-to-Violet bloom; the real camera is the subject the HUD points at. Refuses the flat white-card SaaS page with a single accent.

OWN-WORLD: #F0F0F0 ground with the drifting line grid; glass panels (white 90%→64% gradient, white hairline edge, cool shadow) holding paler wells; Galactic Violet for every action, selection and focus; Bubblegum only in the bloom, HUD nodes, numerals and the one "Pay once" badge; bevelled corners (corner-shape: bevel) on the hero frame, studio, primary buttons and closing card; diagonal hatch marks; black pill for the nav CTA.

Below the hero the world continues: card types and pricing sit in their own bevelled glass panes with the bloom anchored lower right; no kickers above headings.

STORY: The visitor sees the real Sony with HUD callouts tied to its screen, reads the promise, makes a card in the glass studio, and, signed in, works in the same framed pane with the live camera preview haloed beside it.
